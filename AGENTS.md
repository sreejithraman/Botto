# Botto agent guidance

- Before implementation, diagnosis, review, or PR preparation, read
  [project workflow](docs/agents/workflow.md).
- Before naming domain concepts or changing their model, read
  [domain conventions](docs/agents/domain.md) and the relevant glossary and ADRs.
- For product acceptance, read the
  [verification map](docs/agents/verification/index.md). Its recipes and the
  [verification record](docs/verification.md) distinguish coverage from proven results.
- For native view changes, also read [UI behavior](App/Views/AGENTS.md).
- For public-release preparation, read [release checklist](docs/releasing.md).
