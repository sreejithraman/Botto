# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-30)

Initial Botto source release.

- Connect directly to Hermes bots from a native iPhone and iPad app.
- Keep independent chats and drafts, answer requests, and preview or export
  generated files.
- Explore a local demo workspace without a Hermes server.
- Add the Botto brand trio and the shared tagline **Big ideas. Little sidekicks.**
  Tapping the trio switches its greeting between English and Japanese, with a
  happi outfit animation.
- Include experimental encrypted notifications through a Hermes plugin and
  operator-hosted relay. This requires the documented unreleased Hermes patch,
  Apple provisioning, and a configured relay. Signed device delivery is unverified.
- Include contributor checks, a CI workflow, and private security-reporting guidance.
- License Botto under MIT, preserve third-party notices, and credit Hermex by
  Uzair Ansar and Hermes Agent by Nous Research.

See [verification status](docs/verification.md) for tested workflows and known limits.
